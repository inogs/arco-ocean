ARCO-OCEAN

The ocean and sea ice with their atmospheric and hydrological forcing, analysis-ready and cloud-optimized, for machine learning.

Forecast status Products Access Documentation Licence Citation GitHub

ARCO-OCEAN gives the ocean state and its forcing on regular latitude-longitude grids at 0.25°, 0.5° and 1°, one value a day, from 1 January 1993 to 30 June 2026: the ocean and sea ice from the GLORYS12 reanalysis (49 levels, to 5275 m), the atmosphere from ERA5, the rivers' discharge into the ocean from GloFAS. Each coarse resolution is made from the sources, so that every cell keeps the water, ice and river input of the source cells under it. Daily forecasts of the same variables, from the operational systems of the same producers, continue it.

It is built for training machine learning models of the ocean, such as coupled atmosphere-ocean models for subseasonal-to-seasonal forecasts, and is stored as Icechunk repositories on AWS S3, readable without credentials.

Products

One Icechunk repository per product and resolution in the s3://ogs-arco-ocean bucket (region eu-south-1), at <product>/res=<res>, with <res> one of 0p25, 0p5 and 1p0.

ProductPathContent
Historicalhistorical/res=<res>Daily values, 1993-01-01 to 2026-06-30: GLORYS12 (temperature, salinity, currents, sea surface height, sea ice), ERA5 (2 m temperature and dew point, sea-level pressure, 10 m wind, radiation, precipitation), GloFAS (river discharge into the ocean), and the cells' geometry (sea fraction, areas, volumes, bounds).
Statisticsstatistics/res=<res>The climatology and the standard deviation of the anomalies over 1993–2023, by day of the year (groups climatology and anomaly_std).
Forecastforecast/res=<res>The daily 00 UTC runs, 10 lead days, the last five years of runs: GLO12, IFS HRES and the GloFAS forecast, on the historical dataset's grids and variables, with a flag per component.

Access

With xarray and icechunk installed, a repository opens lazily, without credentials:

import icechunk
import xarray as xr

storage = icechunk.s3_storage(
    bucket="ogs-arco-ocean",
    prefix="historical/res=0p25",
    region="eu-south-1",
    anonymous=True,
    network_stream_timeout_seconds=20,
)
repo = icechunk.Repository.open(storage)
dataset = xr.open_zarr(repo.readonly_session("main").store, consolidated=False)

The statistics open one group at a time (group="climatology" or group="anomaly_std"). The ONI tutorial, a marimo notebook, computes the Oceanic Niño Index from the dataset (uvx marimo edit --sandbox tutorials/oni.py).
